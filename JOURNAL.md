# November 5: Designed my schematic layout

  I started trying to figure out how to wire the LCD onto my Arduino, and started figuring out how to take that and design my own schematic (It's literally a LCD and an Arduino, easy peasy!).

  Spoke too soon, I was having trouble figuring out the correct wiring connections for my KiCAD schematic due to the LCD on their site has different names for the pins, and after some digging, I found the 20 pin 128x64 datasheet for the specific piece and was able to correctly design the schematic. 
  
  I then moved on to start my code to get it to display messages, starting with simple phrases like, "Welcome!", "My name is Allie.", and "This is my project stop touching my stuff."
